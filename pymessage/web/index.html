Select a word to analyze its context. Common English function words are hidden from word lists but kept in rate denominators and diversity samples.

Vocabulary over time

Diversity averages complete, non-overlapping 100-word samples. Periods with fewer than 100 words have no diversity score. Counts use lowercase words; URLs, email addresses, and numbers are excluded.

Words by person

Distinctive words have a higher rate with this person than with the other people in this selection. At least three uses combined and 20 words on each side are required. Recipient mix can also change the overall trend.

Tone signals

Percentage of text messages containing each cue. Categories overlap. These English word lists are indicators of wording, not emotion or personality; sarcasm, quotations, slang, and other languages may be missed. Positive/negative cues preceded within three words by a listed negation are skipped.

Words counted

Name mentions

Literal, case-insensitive matches to saved contact names and names you enter; counted once per name per message. First names can match ordinary words or multiple contacts. A match does not establish who you meant, and it may be a greeting to the recipient. Full names take priority over overlapping first names.

Enter one to six words or emoji, separated by commas or spaces. Each selection is analyzed separately. Skin-tone variants stay distinct; ❤ and ❤️ match together.

Usage over time

Occurrences per 1,000 tokens in the selected sent messages. A token is one word or one complete emoji. Emoji-only messages count. Zero means the selection was not used; gaps mean no words or emoji were available.

Context by person

Context uses up to five word or emoji tokens before and after each selection within a sent message, excluding common English function words. Combined emoji (flags, skin tones, keycaps, and joined families) count as one token using bundled Unicode Emoji 17 definitions. Context change compares neighboring-word counts with the previous calendar period (0 = same distribution, 1 = no overlap); it requires five uses and some context on both sides. It measures changing associations, not a proven change in meaning. Weeks start Monday; boundary periods may be partial. URLs, email addresses, numeric tokens, incoming messages, group messages, reactions, and duplicate GUIDs are excluded. Unfiltered person tables show up to 30 people per selection; filter by person to inspect others.
